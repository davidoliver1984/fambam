# FPA-P10-S03 — Conservative face clustering

## Session objective

Implement ADR-0012's identity-free, rebuildable unknown-face clustering
mechanism without beginning identity suggestion, human review workflows,
recognition consent or calibration.

## Work completed

- Added tenant-consistent `face_cluster_generations`, `face_clusters` and
  `face_cluster_members` tables with forced RLS, lifecycle checks and composite
  foreign keys.
- Enforced at most one active generation per Family Space and at most one
  active cluster membership per FaceObservation with partial unique indexes.
- Added deterministic complete-link conservative clustering through the
  provider-neutral `SimilaritySearch` contract. Missing pair evidence never
  becomes a match and a similarity chain cannot merge dissimilar endpoints.
- Made building memberships non-operational and generation activation atomic.
  Activation locks the Family Space, rejects a stale concurrent build,
  deactivates and supersedes only machine-active state, swaps the generation,
  then activates the new memberships inside one transaction.
- Included current machine-cluster members in rebuild input while permanently
  excluding any FaceObservation with membership history in a human-retired
  cluster.
- Extended Family Space teardown to remove the derived cluster hierarchy.
- Added a tenant-bounded operational command that remains disabled behind
  `face_recognition.processing_enabled`. No default numeric clustering cutoff
  exists before S07 calibration.
