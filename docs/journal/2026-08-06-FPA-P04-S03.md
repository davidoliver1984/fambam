# FPA-P04-S03 — Account-to-Person links

**Date:** 2026-08-06

**Status:** Completed — implementation and full stage gate passed

**ADR:** ADR-0006 §§5–6, §§11, §§14–16

## Scope

This stage implements one-to-one account links, bounded self-claim review,
authoritative correction/removal and the requirement that established links
survive account or membership access loss. Relationships, Family Circles and
Person merge remain assigned to S04–S05.

## Implementation

- Added a Family-Space-owned authoritative link with database-enforced unique
  Person and account sides.
- Added separate pending/approved/rejected self-claim records with database
  uniqueness for simultaneous pending claims.
- Member may self-claim from a visible Person. Contributor retains the future
  permission ceiling but cannot treat a guessed ULID as reachability under the
  current no-directory baseline; Contributor and Guest have no S03 claim path.
  Owner and Administrator may list, approve, reject, assign, correct and
  unlink.
- Approval locks and rechecks current Person, active membership and cardinality.
  Direct assignment resolves superseded pending claims.
- Account revocation and membership removal leave established archival links
  unchanged; explicit unlink is separately authorized and audited.
- Added explicit tenant queries, policies, tenant-aware audit events and forced
  Class C RLS for both tables.
- Added typed feature APIs, tenant-aware TanStack Query hooks/mutations and
  role-dependent Person-page controls without direct transport access in React
  pages or presentational components.
